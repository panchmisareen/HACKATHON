# ♻️ PW IOI Campus Circular Thrift Club & Second-Hand Exchange
**Better habits. Lighter footprint. — Student Wellness & Sustainability Platform**

---

## 📌 About Circular Thrift Club
The **EcoPulse Circular Thrift Club** is a peer-to-peer campus micro-economy where students at the PW Institute of Innovation (PW IOI) Noida (First Floor, Silver Springs, Sector 126) buy, sell, and donate pre-loved textbooks, electronics, dorm appliances, and apparel.

By choosing pre-loved items instead of buying new, students directly eliminate the upstream raw material extraction, synthetic chemical dyeing, semiconductor fabrication, and global freight transport emissions.

## 📝 How to List Your Own Item on EcoPulse
1. Open the EcoPulse Web App at [http://127.0.0.1:5173/app/thrift](http://127.0.0.1:5173/app/thrift).
2. Click **"+ List Your Pre-Loved Item"**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Item Title** & **Category**
   - **Your Price (₹)** *(Set to 0 for Free Campus Donation)*
   - **Condition** *(Brand New / Like New / Good / Fair)*
   - **Hostel / Location Pickup Point**
   - **Your Contact Info** *(WhatsApp / Telegram / Email)*
4. The EcoPulse Carbon Engine will automatically calculate and stamp your listing with its **Certified Avoided $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ Badge**.
5. Claim your **+35 Green Points** instantly upon publishing!
